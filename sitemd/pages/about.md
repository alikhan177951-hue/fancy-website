# A one-stop excavation shop in Altona Meadows

Locally owned and operated. David runs the machines, the quotes, and the clean-up.

![Locally owned DB Bobcat plant](/media/gallery/abbyy-1.jpg)

DB Bobcat and Tipper Hire specialises in site cleaning, soil removal, rock removal, rubbish removal, concrete removal, small demolition, and concrete cutting. The owner-operator has years in the industry and is particular about reliable, efficient excavation.

Projects are scoped with the client so the solution matches the block. From site preparation through demolition and clean-up, the same person who quoted is usually the person on site.

Customer satisfaction, safety, and environmental responsibility sit together: recycle when it is possible, leave the site tidy, and keep clients informed.
