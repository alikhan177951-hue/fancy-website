# Eight ways to move the site

Residential and commercial excavation across western Melbourne. Hourly hire plus floatage, or a fixed quote after a look.

{#site-preparation}
## Site Preparation

![Site preparation](/media/gallery/site-preparation-1.jpg)

Get the job off the ground with excavation and levelling. David matches the machine to the block — tight residential access or larger commercial pads.

{#rock-removal}
## Rock Removal

![Rock removal](/media/gallery/rock-removal.jpg)

Rock of any size, removed without drama. Hire can be hourly plus floatage, or quoted as a fixed job once David has seen the site.

{#concrete-cutting}
## Concrete Cutting

![Concrete cutting](/media/gallery/concrete-cutting.jpg)

A range of concrete cutters for tight access and hard ground. Used to break up old footings, concrete, and brickwork before removal.

{#site-clean}
## Site Clean

![Site clean](/media/gallery/site-clean.jpg)

Post-construction clean-up: excavate footings to builder or engineer specs, cart spoils in tippers, and finish with a final trim.

{#rubbish-removal}
## Rubbish Removal

![Rubbish removal](/media/gallery/rubbish.jpeg)

Builders’ waste and spoils go in tippers from 2 tonne through to 12 tonne 6-wheelers. Material is recycled where possible — separating loads on site helps keep tipping fees down.

{#small-demolition}
## Small Demolition

![Small demolition](/media/gallery/small-demolition.jpg)

Sheds and other small structures come down as a one-person, owner-operated job with the emphasis on safety and a clean finish. Augers from 150 mm to 600 mm, rock augers, and extensions are also available when the job needs holes rather than a knockdown.

{#soil-removal}
## Soil Removal

![Soil removal](/media/gallery/soil-removal.jpg)

Soil out, whatever the volume or access. Complimentary job-site inspections so the right machine turns up the first time.

{#concrete-removal}
## Concrete Removal

![Concrete removal](/media/gallery/a-8.jpeg)

Driveways, footpaths, old footings, and brickwork removed with machinery sized for tight lots and hard ground.
