DentReady Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Contact: alikhan177951@gmail.com

DentReady (“the App”) is an educational practice tool for the Australian Dental Council (ADC) Written Examination. DentReady is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Australian Dental Council.

Summary

DentReady does not collect personal data. Practice progress, mock results and settings stay on your device. We do not operate accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising trackers or cloud sync for study data in the current version.

Information we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No name, email, phone number or payment details inside the App
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking SDKs
  • No upload of mock answers, scores or study progress to our servers

Information stored on your device

The App stores practice state locally (for example completed attempts, flags and resume state) using on-device storage such as AsyncStorage / SQLite. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

Internet use

The App may open official ADC documents or image credit source pages in your browser when you choose those links. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies. DentReady does not transmit your study data when you open those links.

Children

DentReady is intended for adult dental professionals preparing for an exam. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
